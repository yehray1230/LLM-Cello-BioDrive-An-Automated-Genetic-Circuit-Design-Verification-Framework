# Archived Project Closeout

**Closeout date:** 2026-08-30

**Final status:** `CLOSED_UNSUCCESSFUL_ARCHIVED`

**Project stage:** `0.x research preview`

## Outcome first

This project explored whether a natural-language-to-genetic-circuit workflow
could combine generative agents, deterministic checks, external-tool
boundaries, simulations, and explicit evidence governance.

The bounded final closeout completed **zero real provider-to-Cello full-path
projects**. The repository should therefore be read as an archived software and
research-method exploration, not as a validated genetic-circuit design system.

The most defensible outcome is the inspectable engineering record: software
contracts, evidence classification, fail-closed attempt accounting, a separate
historical mapping-only result, and an independently identified integration
defect that triggered the stopping rule.

## Why the final integration gate failed

The intended final workflow selected Gemini 2.5 Flash as the provider candidate.
The accepted identity required the provider, transport model, and reported model
to match the frozen values exactly.

An independent zero-call review found that the canonical service path could
accept a caller-supplied runner with an arbitrary provider identity and absent
transport and reported-model identities. Other accounting and fail-closed
controls passed their local checks, but the canonical identity boundary was not
fully enforced. Because both permitted implementation revisions had already
been used, the contract required a terminal No-Go rather than another repair.

Provider qualification and the three planned real projects were consequently
not attempted. No provider call, paid call, current mapping run, Podman action,
or retry was performed by the final closeout protocol.

## What was implemented

The repository contains an inspectable research-preview implementation of:

- natural-language intent and structured regulatory-logic representations;
- multi-agent proposal, translation, critique, and repair paths;
- Boolean logic and Cello-compatible Verilog generation;
- mock-versus-external provenance boundaries;
- deterministic validation and evidence-aware failure states;
- simplified ODE, stochastic, robustness, resource, sequence, host, readiness,
  and export-oriented screening;
- machine-readable evidence and claim-decision records;
- fail-closed reservation, attempt, and accounting controls in the reviewed
  integration work.

Implemented surface area is not the same as demonstrated end-to-end capability.
The final closeout deliberately keeps those categories separate.

## What was not demonstrated

The project did not demonstrate:

- a successful real provider-to-Cello full path;
- provider qualification;
- two successful real projects or a repeatable success rate;
- biological validity or wet-lab function;
- complete plasmid buildability;
- calibrated in-vivo prediction;
- model or multi-agent superiority;
- production or publication readiness.

## Why the project stopped

The architecture and evidence requirements had expanded beyond the value of
additional implementation under the user's intended learning and portfolio
goals. The final protocol therefore used a fixed revision budget and an
independent review gate. Once the remaining canonical identity defect was found
and the revision budget was exhausted, stopping preserved a clearer and more
credible result than continuing indefinitely.

## Reusable lessons

- Green local tests do not replace adversarial checks at the canonical service
  boundary.
- Provider identity, attempt accounting, retries, and external-tool actions need
  explicit, shared, fail-closed authority.
- Computational, fixture, mock, external-tool, biological, and wet-lab evidence
  must remain separate.
- A documented No-Go can be a valid project result when it preserves the exact
  failure, limits the claim, and prevents unsupported execution.
- The strongest portfolio value is problem framing, inspectable engineering,
  evidence discipline, and honest failure analysis—not a claim of a mature
  biological CAD system.
